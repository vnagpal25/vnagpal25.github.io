A long-term memory engine for LLM agents. Aether stores conversation history as a semantic hypergraph (built on XGI) and retrieves from it with dual-graph search kernels. It reaches 94.16% accuracy on the LoCoMo benchmark and cuts P99 retrieval latency by 60%.
